Aloe Vera

Common Names: Aloe vera, Ghritkumari (घृतकुमारी) (Hindi), Burn Plant, Miracle Plant, Barbados Aloe

Habitat: Thrives in warm, dry regions like India, Africa, and the Mediterranean

Medicinal Uses: Heals burns, improves digestion, boosts immunity, and promotes hair health

Cultivation: Grows in sandy, well-drained soil, requires minimal watering and sunlight, propagated through offshoots, and harvested after 8–10 months

Tulsi

Common Names: Tulsi, Holy Basil, Ocimum sanctum

Habitat:Native to India and Southeast Asia; grows well in tropical and subtropical climates.

Medicinal Uses: Boosts immunity, relieves stress, supports respiratory health, aids digestion, and has antibacterial properties.

Cultivation: Grows well in well-drained soil with ample sunlight; requires regular watering and can be propagated through seeds or cuttings.

Ahwagandha

Common Names: Ashwagandha, Indian Ginseng, Withania somnifera

Habitat: Native to India, the Middle East, and parts of Africa; thrives in dry regions with moderate temperatures.

Medicinal Uses: Reduces stress and anxiety, boosts energy levels, enhances brain function, improves immunity, and supports muscle strength.

Cultivation: Grows well in well-drained, sandy or loamy soil; requires a warm climate with minimal rainfall; propagated through seeds and harvested in 5–6 months.

Cardamom

Common Names: Cardamom, Elaichi (Hindi), Green Cardamom, True Cardamom

Habitat: Native to India, Sri Lanka, and Southeast Asia; thrives in tropical rainforests with high humidity and shade.

Medicinal Uses: Aids digestion, relieves acidity, freshens breath, improves respiratory health, and has antioxidant properties.

Cultivation: Grows best in warm, humid climates with well-drained, loamy soil; requires partial shade and regular watering; harvested after 2-3 years.

Common Names: Cinnamon, Dalchini (Hindi), Ceylon Cinnamon, True Cinnamon

Habitat: Native to Sri Lanka, India, and Southeast Asia; grows in tropical forests with warm, humid climates.
